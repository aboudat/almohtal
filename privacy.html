Privacy Policy for Al Mohtal (المحتال)

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Al Mohtal party game ("the app") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Al Mohtal does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and needs no account, no sign in, and no network connection to play.

Local Storage

The app saves the player names you type, your language choice, and your game settings on your device using local storage, so you do not have to re-enter them next time. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted here.
